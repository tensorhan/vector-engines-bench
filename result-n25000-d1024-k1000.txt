How `Index (MB)` was obtained, per row:
- Raw TurboVec (4-bit): exact (len(index.to_bytes()))
- Raw TurboVec (2-bit): exact (len(index.to_bytes()))
- FAISS IndexFlatIP (FP32): analytical (n*dim*4 bytes, exact flat layout)
- FAISS IndexPQFastScan (4-bit): analytical (m=dim x 4-bit codes + codebooks) - approximate: codebooks are trained on the dataset
- HNSW (hnswlib FP32): analytical (n * (dim*4 + 32*4) bytes) - graph adds ~8% over raw FP32 vectors; ef_search=64
- LangChain InMemory (FP32): analytical (n*dim*4 bytes; Python object overhead is in Py Heap) - brute force in interpreted Python; vectors are stored as float lists
- LangChain + TurboVec (4-bit): exact (len(index.to_bytes())); text/metadata side-car is in Py Heap
- LlamaIndex Simple (FP32): analytical (n*dim*4 bytes; Python object overhead is in Py Heap) - brute force in interpreted Python; TextNode metadata is extra
- LlamaIndex + TurboVec (4-bit): exact (len(index.to_bytes()))

Notes:
- Timings are the median of 10 runs and 3 builds per engine; `p50` and `Build` carry the stdev across them.
- Compression is raw FP32 payload (97.7 MB = N*dim*4) divided by `Index (MB)`; 1.0x means the engine stores the vectors uncompressed.
- Recall@10 is evaluated against exact brute-force Float32 cosine ground truth (Q @ D.T).
- `Build (s)` is time until the index can answer a query and `Train (s)` is the part of it spent fitting codebooks. They are not comparable across engines: FAISS flat copies floats, PQ trains codebooks, TurboVec quantizes, HNSW builds a graph.
- `Py Heap (MB)` is the Python-side `tracemalloc` peak over the whole build, measured the same way for every engine. It tracks framework object overhead, not index size, and for the two FP32 baselines it includes the rows returned by the embedding stub.
- `*` marks engines that are exact by construction (brute force over the same vectors the ground truth came from), so their recall must be 100% if the plumbing is correct.
- Measured p50 spread on this run: slowest FP32 framework 854.2 ms vs fastest TurboVec 0.373 ms (2,289x).
- Measured TurboVec Recall@10: 88.8% to 96.7% against exact FP32 ground truth.
- Closest timings: Raw TurboVec (4-bit) (0.822 ms) and LangChain + TurboVec (4-bit) (0.860 ms) differ by 0.037 ms against a spread of 0.002 ms - outside their spread, so the gap holds.
- Exact engines were probed with 10 queries: brute force over these same vectors is the ground truth, so more queries cannot change the answer.
